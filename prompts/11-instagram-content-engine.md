# Phase 11 — Instagram and Content Engine

## Objective

Build a repeatable distribution system around Promptya rather than treating Instagram as an occasional manual posting channel.

## Strategy

Use content to reinforce the core loop:

**Discover → Create → Share → Earn → Return**

Content pillars may include:
- prompt demonstrations
- before/after generations
- AI Studio outputs
- challenge announcements
- challenge winners
- useful prompt-engineering education
- developer/AI tool education
- community highlights

## Required Work

Define:
- content pillars
- posting cadence
- reusable templates
- CTA strategy
- challenge promotion flow
- UGC reuse policy
- attribution rules
- measurement

Create an operational content workflow that a small team can maintain.

## Hard Prohibitions

- Do not automate spam.
- Do not use fake engagement.
- Do not scrape private user data.
- Do not promise sponsorship results without evidence.
- Do not make social content the product itself.

## Acceptance Criteria

There is a repeatable content calendar and production workflow.

Each content type has:
- purpose
- audience
- CTA
- source material
- measurement metric

## Verification

Review the workflow against available product analytics and community activity.

## Final Report

Report the content system, initial calendar, measurement plan, and manual/automated boundaries.
