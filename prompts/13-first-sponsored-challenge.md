# Phase 13 — First Sponsored Challenge

## Objective

Prepare and execute the first sponsor-backed Promptya challenge using a controlled campaign model.

## Sponsor Value Proposition

A sponsor may provide:
- API credits
- cloud/server credits
- subscriptions
- product access
- prizes
- technical resources

Promptya can provide:
- creator participation
- challenge distribution
- UGC
- product/model exposure
- social distribution
- measurable campaign activity

The campaign loop is:

**Sponsor → Prize/Credits → Challenge → Creators → Content → Distribution → Users → Creators**

## Required Investigation

Before contacting or integrating a sponsor, define:
- target sponsor category
- campaign objective
- target audience
- reward budget
- eligibility
- verification method
- campaign dates
- sponsor deliverables
- Promptya deliverables
- metrics
- contingency plan

## Campaign Design

Create a challenge that:
- has a clear creative brief
- has a measurable reward
- has server-controlled eligibility
- has a fair verification process
- can be promoted through Promptya and social channels
- does not require unsafe or misleading claims

## Operational Requirements

Track:
- participants
- submissions
- verified submissions
- rewards issued
- generated content
- social reach where measurable
- new signups
- activation
- sponsor-specific conversions where measurable

## Hard Prohibitions

- Do not promise guaranteed reach or sales.
- Do not fake engagement.
- Do not manipulate winner selection unfairly.
- Do not grant rewards outside the defined rules.
- Do not build sponsor billing infrastructure unless explicitly required.
- Do not disclose private participant data to sponsors without a valid legal/product basis.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Sponsor proposal is concrete.
- Campaign rules are explicit.
- Reward economics are safe.
- Verification is operationally feasible.
- Metrics can be measured.
- A post-campaign report can be produced.

## Final Report

Include campaign structure, sponsor deliverables, Promptya deliverables, measurement plan, risks, and post-campaign evaluation criteria.
